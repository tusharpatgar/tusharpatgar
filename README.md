## 🧠 About Me

Data Engineer with **1.4 years of hands-on experience** building enterprise-scale **ETL/ELT pipelines** and cloud-based data solutions across **FinTech** and **analytics** domains. I specialize in designing and delivering reliable data infrastructure — from **CDC ingestion** and **medallion (Bronze–Silver–Gold) architecture** to **incremental processing** and **data quality validation**.

I work primarily in **Python, PySpark, SQL and T-SQL**, and I'm comfortable end-to-end across the modern data stack: **AWS (S3, Glue, DMS, Lambda, Redshift, CloudWatch)**, **Databricks + Delta Lake**, **Apache Airflow**, **dbt**, **Snowflake**, and **SSIS**. My focus is building data products that are accurate, performant, and maintainable — and shipping them safely through production cutovers and migrations.

> *"Building reliable data pipelines, one dataset at a time."*
